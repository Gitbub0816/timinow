CLINIC PARTICIPATION TERMS

Independent practice, accountable capacity.

1. Independent status. Your clinic remains, at all times, an independent veterinary provider and is solely responsible for its own licensure, staffing, medical judgment, triage, treatment, recordkeeping, informed consent, billing, insurance handling, and compliance with the veterinary practice act and all other law applicable in your jurisdiction. Nothing in these terms creates a partnership, joint venture, agency relationship, or employment relationship between ClearKey Solutions, LLC and your clinic. If your clinic is staffed by a registered, licensed, or certified veterinary technician rather than a licensed veterinarian, you must disclose that fact to Tími NOW at onboarding, keep that disclosure current, and remain solely responsible for operating within the scope of practice your state's veterinary practice act permits and for maintaining your own supervision arrangements. Owner-supplied medication and allergy information is furnished to your clinic as convenience information only and does not substitute for your own history-taking or medical records. Your clinic must keep its facility, capability, hours, deposit policy, and reported capacity accurate, and must promptly offer availability, decline, or allow a request to expire.

2. Availability responses. An availability response your clinic submits is a temporary operational offer, valid only for the displayed hold period. A pet owner may compare up to five such offers concurrently. Once a pet owner selects a clinic, that clinic alone receives confirmation, and every other outstanding offer is automatically released. A response to an emergency-flagged request remains subject to your independent triage and does not reserve any priority in the order of examination.

3. Intake information and credentials. Your personnel may access intake information transmitted through the Service solely to evaluate and provide the requested care, must safeguard their credentials against unauthorized use, and must comply with applicable privacy and data-security law in handling that information. Your clinic must not represent a status displayed by the Service as a guarantee of any particular examination time.

4. Stripe Connect. Where Stripe Connect is enabled for your account, you must complete Stripe's onboarding process, accept the Stripe Connected Account Agreement then in effect, and authorize the payment, refund, fee, and payout flows disclosed to you. Tími NOW furnishes a policy snapshot and monthly settlement record; your clinic remains solely responsible for its own tax obligations and for issuing its own veterinary invoices.

5. Fees. Tími NOW charges a fee per completed connection: the pet owner pays the disclosed booking fee at confirmation, and your clinic pays the disclosed clinic fee, invoiced monthly for connections that reached verified completion. A Founding Clinic pays no such fee for so long as it remains participating and in good standing. The fee is a platform charge, not a veterinary charge, and is never billed to insurance. A declined, expired, or cancelled request carries no fee under this section.

6. Paw It Forward sponsorship. Where a booking is sponsored by the Paw It Forward Fund, your Tími NOW referral fee for that booking is zero dollars and the pet owner pays no Tími NOW fee. The patient remains responsible for your normal deposit and veterinary charges. Tími NOW does not disclose to your clinic why a booking is sponsored, and your clinic must not ask a patient about it: income, benefits, hardship reasons, and supporting documents are never shared with clinics.
