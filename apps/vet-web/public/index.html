LEGAL CENTER

Clinic participation terms.

CLINIC PARTICIPATION TERMS

Independent practice, accountable capacity.

Participating clinics remain independent veterinary providers and are solely responsible for licensure, staffing, medical judgment, triage, treatment, records, informed consent, billing, insurance, and regulatory compliance. Clinics must keep facility, capability, hours, deposit policy, and capacity reports accurate and must promptly offer availability, decline, or allow requests to expire.

An availability response is a temporary operational offer for the displayed hold period. The customer may compare up to five offers. Only the clinic selected by the customer receives confirmation; all other offers are automatically released. Emergency-intake responses remain subject to independent triage and do not reserve examination priority.

Clinic users may access intake information only for evaluating and providing requested care, must protect credentials, and must follow applicable privacy and security obligations. A clinic may not present a Tími status as a guarantee of examination time.

Where Stripe Connect is enabled, the clinic must complete Stripe onboarding, accept the applicable Stripe Connected Account Agreement, and authorize the disclosed payment, refund, fee, and payout flows. Tími provides a policy snapshot and monthly settlement record; the clinic remains responsible for its own taxes and veterinary invoices.

Tími NOW charges a fee per completed connection: the pet owner pays the disclosed booking fee at confirmation, and your clinic pays the disclosed clinic fee, invoiced monthly for connections that reached verified completion. Founding clinics pay $0 while participating and in good standing. The fee is a platform charge, not a veterinary charge, and is never billed to insurance. Declined, expired, and cancelled requests carry no fee.

Where a booking is sponsored by the Paw It Forward Fund, your Tími NOW referral fee for that booking is $0 and the pet owner pays no Tími NOW fee. The patient remains responsible for your normal deposit and veterinary charges. Tími NOW does not disclose to a clinic why a booking is sponsored, and a clinic must not ask a patient about it: income, benefits, hardship reasons, and supporting documents are never shared with clinics.
