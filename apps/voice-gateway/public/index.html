Voice gateway

This Worker holds no customer-facing or clinic-facing screens. It exists so a clinic with nobody watching a console still gets asked, by phone, whether they can take a patient.

What this is

When a customer's care search fans out to nearby clinics, most of them have no one looking at a screen. timinow-voice drains that queue every minute, places a short automated call, and lets the clinic answer with the phone's keypad — press 1 to confirm they can see the patient, 2 to decline. Pressing 1 has the identical effect as a staffer clicking "accept" on the console: it creates the same clinic offer, honors the same five-offer cap, and respects the same expiry.

There is nothing to sign in to here and nothing to click. The veterinary console at timinow-vet is where a clinic manages requests by hand; this Worker is the unattended fallback for the calls that would otherwise go unanswered.
