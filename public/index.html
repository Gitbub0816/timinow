    TERMS OF USE

    Using Tími

    You must be at least 18 and able to enter a contract. Tími provides technology for locating veterinary facilities, displaying reported intake capacity, and sending a structured intake request. Tími is not the clinic, veterinarian, merchant for veterinary treatment, insurer, or emergency service.

    No promise of care

    A listing, status, estimated wait, acceptance, or arrival window is not an appointment guarantee. Capacity can change when critical patients arrive. The clinic independently decides whether and when to examine or treat an animal and sets all clinical and billing decisions.

    Your responsibilities

    Provide accurate, current information; do not impersonate another person; do not misuse the service; and contact the clinic directly if the animal worsens. We may suspend abusive, fraudulent, unsafe, or unlawful use.

    Service and liability

    The service is provided on an “as available” basis. To the maximum extent allowed by law, ClearKey Solutions, LLC is not liable for independent clinic conduct, clinical outcomes, inaccurate clinic reports, travel decisions, or indirect or consequential loss. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be waived. California law governs these terms, with venue in Alameda County, California.

    PRIVACY POLICY + NOTICE AT COLLECTION

    What we collect and why

    We collect identifiers and contact details; pet details and the concern you describe, including any medications or allergies you choose to record (optional, and shared with the clinics your request reaches); approximate or precise location when you allow it; clinics contacted, offers received, your selected clinic, and request history; deposit and transaction status; device, security, and diagnostic logs; and clinic-user and organization information.

    We use this information to operate intake routing, show nearby capacity, share a structured request with up to 30 matching participating clinics, collect and display up to five active availability offers, send transactional updates, process deposits, prevent abuse, secure and improve the service, comply with law, and support users. We do not use pet-concern information to provide a diagnosis.

    Who receives it

    The structured intake may be disclosed to participating clinics contacted for your search, even when you ultimately choose a different clinic. Your selection and payment information are sent only as needed to confirm the clinic you choose. Information may also be disclosed to infrastructure, authentication, communications, analytics, security, and payment processors working for us; professional advisers; and authorities when legally required. Stripe receives payment information under its own privacy terms. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

    First-party analytics

    We measure how our own websites are used with a first-party analytics system that we operate ourselves. Each recorded event contains only an event name, the page it happened on, which surface it came from (customer site, veterinary console, or operator console), a coarse device class, a coarse country, and a daily-rotating anonymous hash. This system sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, is not used for advertising, and the information is never sold or shared; because the hash rotates every day, it cannot follow a visitor across days or across sites. Analytics records are retained for no more than 13 months. Because this measurement sets no cookies and holds no persistent identifier, no consent banner is required for it.

    Location and retention

    Location is used only when requested to find nearby care and estimate travel. You may use the demonstration area or browser controls instead. Intake and transaction records are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for operations, disputes, accounting, safety, and legal obligations; security logs may be retained for a shorter operational period.

    Your choices

    You may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of applicable personal information by emailing privacy@clearkey.solutions. We verify requests and may retain information where an exception applies. We honor legally applicable browser Global Privacy Control signals. Tími is for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

    VETERINARY SAFETY NOTICE

    Tími routes intake. It does not practice veterinary medicine.

    Finding emergency hospitals

    Tími’s emergency list is not limited to participating clinics, because the nearest emergency hospital often is not one. Listings outside the network come from third-party map data, including their names, addresses and telephone numbers. Tími has not verified that they exist as listed, are open, are equipped for your animal, or will accept a patient, and no care request is sent to them — the list is somewhere to drive, not a booking and not a recommendation. Call before you travel where you can.

    Providers staffed by a veterinary technician

    Some participating providers are staffed by a registered, licensed, or certified veterinary technician rather than a veterinarian. Where that is the case, Tími labels the provider before you choose it. A veterinary technician works under a veterinarian’s supervision and, under state practice acts, may not diagnose, prognose, prescribe, or perform surgery. Those providers are listed for minor concerns; anything that may require a diagnosis or a treatment decision should be taken to a veterinarian. The staffing label is set by Tími from information the provider supplies at onboarding and is not a verification of any individual’s credential, licence status, or scope of practice, which remain the provider’s responsibility.

    Medications and allergies you record

    Any medications or allergies you add to a pet profile are optional, are stored as you type them, and are shared with the clinics your care request reaches. Tími is not a medical record system: nothing in that field comes from a veterinarian, none of it is verified, and no clinic may rely on it in place of its own history-taking. Keep it current, and confirm it directly with the treating clinic. Do not record anything you do not want shared with the clinics contacted for a request.

    Tími does not practise

    Tími does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend treatment, create a veterinarian-client-patient relationship, or replace clinical triage. Structured questions and red-flag routing are operational safety rules, not medical advice. Only the independent veterinary team can evaluate your animal.

    Do not wait for an app response if your animal may be in immediate danger. Travel to the nearest appropriate emergency-capable veterinary facility while someone calls ahead. If poisoning may have occurred, contact a veterinarian or a recognized animal poison-control service immediately.

    Displayed wait times and capacity are reports or estimates with a visible source and age. They may change without notice, and critical patients may be seen first.

    DEPOSITS, REFUNDS + CANCELLATIONS

    The clinic’s policy is shown before payment.

    Some clinics require an arrival deposit after accepting a request; others collect no payment through Tími. When required, the exact amount, free-cancellation window, refund/no-show rule, policy version, and clinic name are shown before you authorize payment.

    Unless the displayed clinic policy says otherwise, the full deposit is credited against that clinic’s final invoice. The clinic bills all remaining veterinary charges directly and decides insurance handling. Tími does not submit veterinary insurance claims. Card processing is provided by Stripe. Tími’s platform fee is settled with the clinic and is not a veterinary charge billed to insurance.

    Refunds follow the policy snapshot you accepted, subject to applicable law. To dispute a deposit, contact billing@clearkey.solutions and the clinic. Cancelling a Tími request does not itself cancel care already arranged directly with a clinic.

    Tími’s own fee is separate from the clinic’s deposit. It is itemized on the confirmation screen before you authorize payment, together with any optional Paw It Forward contribution you choose to add — see the Service Fee section. Where the clinic deposit is collected as its own charge rather than in the same payment, the number of charges and the purpose of each is shown before you confirm.

    SERVICE FEE

    What Tími charges, and to whom.

    A pet owner pays Tími the disclosed booking fee for a completed connection. The amount is shown and itemized on the confirmation screen before payment is authorized. A participating clinic separately pays Tími the disclosed clinic fee for the same completed connection, invoiced to the clinic. Founding clinics pay $0 while they remain participating and in good standing under the clinic agreement.

    The service fee is a platform charge for intake routing and settlement. It is not a veterinary charge, is not part of the clinic’s invoice for care, and is not billed to insurance. It applies only to completed connections — declined, expired, and cancelled requests carry no service fee.

    Where a booking is sponsored by the Paw It Forward Fund, the pet owner pays no Tími fee and the clinic pays no Tími referral fee for that booking. The clinic’s own deposit and veterinary charges are unaffected and remain the pet owner’s responsibility. See the Paw It Forward program terms.

    An optional Paw It Forward contribution may be added to a booking payment. It is never added by default, is itemized separately from the fee before payment, and forms no part of the service fee.

    PAW IT FORWARD PROGRAM TERMS

    What a contribution does, and what it does not do.

    The Tími NOW Paw It Forward Fund uses community contributions to cover Tími NOW access fees for pet owners whose financial hardship has been independently verified under Tími’s published eligibility rules. Contributions support eligible Tími NOW access. They do not buy a named recipient’s service, and they do not pay veterinary treatment, medication, clinic deposits, or any other charge billed by a veterinary practice.

    This contribution is not represented by TímiNOW as tax deductible. Tími NOW is operated by ClearKey Solutions, LLC, a for-profit company, and claims no tax-exempt or nonprofit status. You receive an ordinary payment receipt showing the merchant, amount, date, payment method, and support contact. It is not a receipt for a deductible gift, and no deduction should be claimed on the basis of it.

    A contribution is voluntary and is not required to book, to be matched with a clinic, or to receive any Tími NOW service. Contributions are pooled: they are not held for, promised to, or directed at any particular pet, pet owner, clinic, or booking, and a contributor cannot select a recipient or influence an eligibility decision.

    Funds are held as a restricted-purpose balance and are consumed only when a sponsored connection is actually completed. Amounts reserved for a booking that is cancelled or that expires return to the fund. Tími NOW may decline, limit, or pause assistance where program budget rules, fraud controls, or applicable law require it.

    Eligibility is decided by versioned rules applied to independently verified evidence, not by a subjective judgement about any applicant. A decision that Tími could not verify hardship is not a finding of dishonesty. Applicants may write to hardship@timinow.pet for human review affecting future bookings.

    Contribution refunds are handled case by case at billing@clearkey.solutions and follow applicable law and card-network rules. Payment processing is provided by Stripe under its own terms. Recurring contributions are not offered.

    Information about an applicant’s income, benefits, hardship reason, or supporting documents is never disclosed to a clinic or to a contributor. A clinic is told only that a booking is sponsored and that its Tími referral fee for that booking is $0.

    CLINIC PARTICIPATION TERMS

    Independent practice, accountable capacity.

    Participating clinics remain independent veterinary providers and are solely responsible for licensure, staffing, medical judgment, triage, treatment, records, informed consent, billing, insurance, and regulatory compliance. A provider staffed by a veterinary technician rather than a veterinarian must tell Tími so at onboarding and must keep that accurate; the provider remains responsible for operating within its state practice act and for its own supervision arrangements. Owner-supplied medications and allergies are shared as convenience information only and do not replace the clinic’s own history-taking or records. Clinics must keep facility, capability, hours, deposit policy, and capacity reports accurate and must promptly offer availability, decline, or allow requests to expire.

    An availability response is a temporary operational offer for the displayed hold period. The customer may compare up to five offers. Only the clinic selected by the customer receives confirmation; all other offers are automatically released. Emergency-intake responses remain subject to independent triage and do not reserve examination priority.

    Clinic users may access intake information only for evaluating and providing requested care, must protect credentials, and must follow applicable privacy and security obligations. A clinic may not present a Tími status as a guarantee of examination time.

    Where Stripe Connect is enabled, the clinic must complete Stripe onboarding, accept the applicable Stripe Connected Account Agreement, and authorize the disclosed payment, refund, fee, and payout flows. Tími provides a policy snapshot and monthly settlement record; the clinic remains responsible for its own taxes and veterinary invoices.

    Tími’s fee is charged per completed connection: the pet owner pays the disclosed booking fee at confirmation and the clinic pays the disclosed clinic fee, invoiced to the clinic. Founding clinics pay $0 while participating and in good standing. See the Service Fee section.

    Where a booking is sponsored by the Paw It Forward Fund, the clinic’s Tími referral fee for that booking is $0 and the clinic is told only that the booking is sponsored. A clinic is never given the pet owner’s income, benefit type, hardship reason, supporting documents, or assistance application, and must not ask a patient for them. The patient remains responsible for the clinic’s normal deposit and veterinary charges.
