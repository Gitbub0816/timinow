    PRIVACY POLICY + NOTICE AT COLLECTION

    What we collect and why

    We collect identifiers and contact details; pet details and the concern you describe, including any medications or allergies you choose to record (optional, and shared with the clinics your request reaches); approximate or precise location when you allow it; clinics contacted, offers received, your selected clinic, and request history; deposit and transaction status; device, security, and diagnostic logs; and clinic-user and organization information.

    We use this information to operate intake routing, show nearby capacity, share a structured request with up to 30 matching participating clinics, collect and display up to five active availability offers, send transactional updates, process deposits, prevent abuse, secure and improve the service, comply with law, and support users. We do not use pet-concern information to provide a diagnosis.

    Who receives it

    The structured intake may be disclosed to participating clinics contacted for your search, even when you ultimately choose a different clinic. Your selection and payment information are sent only as needed to confirm the clinic you choose. Information may also be disclosed to infrastructure, authentication, communications, analytics, security, and payment processors working for us; professional advisers; and authorities when legally required. Stripe receives payment information under its own privacy terms. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

    First-party analytics

    We measure how our own websites are used with a first-party analytics system that we operate ourselves. Each recorded event contains only an event name, the page it happened on, which surface it came from (customer site, veterinary console, or operator console), a coarse device class, a coarse country, and a daily-rotating anonymous hash. This system sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, is not used for advertising, and the information is never sold or shared; because the hash rotates every day, it cannot follow a visitor across days or across sites. Analytics records are retained for no more than 13 months. Because this measurement sets no cookies and holds no persistent identifier, no consent banner is required for it.

    Location and retention

    Location is used only when requested to find nearby care and estimate travel. You may use the demonstration area or browser controls instead. Intake and transaction records are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for operations, disputes, accounting, safety, and legal obligations; security logs may be retained for a shorter operational period.

    Your choices

    You may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of applicable personal information by emailing privacy@clearkey.solutions. We verify requests and may retain information where an exception applies. We honor legally applicable browser Global Privacy Control signals. Tími is for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

    VETERINARY SAFETY NOTICE

    Tími routes intake. It does not practice veterinary medicine.

    Finding emergency hospitals

    Tími’s emergency list is not limited to participating clinics, because the nearest emergency hospital often is not one. Listings outside the network come from third-party map data, including their names, addresses and telephone numbers. Tími has not verified that they exist as listed, are open, are equipped for your animal, or will accept a patient, and no care request is sent to them — the list is somewhere to drive, not a booking and not a recommendation. Call before you travel where you can.

    Providers staffed by a veterinary technician

    Some participating providers are staffed by a registered, licensed, or certified veterinary technician rather than a veterinarian. Where that is the case, Tími labels the provider before you choose it. A veterinary technician works under a veterinarian’s supervision and, under state practice acts, may not diagnose, prognose, prescribe, or perform surgery. Those providers are listed for minor concerns; anything that may require a diagnosis or a treatment decision should be taken to a veterinarian. The staffing label is set by Tími from information the provider supplies at onboarding and is not a verification of any individual’s credential, licence status, or scope of practice, which remain the provider’s responsibility.

    Medications and allergies you record

    Any medications or allergies you add to a pet profile are optional, are stored as you type them, and are shared with the clinics your care request reaches. Tími is not a medical record system: nothing in that field comes from a veterinarian, none of it is verified, and no clinic may rely on it in place of its own history-taking. Keep it current, and confirm it directly with the treating clinic. Do not record anything you do not want shared with the clinics contacted for a request.

    Tími does not practise

    Tími does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend treatment, create a veterinarian-client-patient relationship, or replace clinical triage. Structured questions and red-flag routing are operational safety rules, not medical advice. Only the independent veterinary team can evaluate your animal.

    Do not wait for an app response if your animal may be in immediate danger. Travel to the nearest appropriate emergency-capable veterinary facility while someone calls ahead. If poisoning may have occurred, contact a veterinarian or a recognized animal poison-control service immediately.

    Displayed wait times and capacity are reports or estimates with a visible source and age. They may change without notice, and critical patients may be seen first.

    DEPOSITS, REFUNDS + CANCELLATIONS

    The clinic’s policy is shown before payment.

    Some clinics require an arrival deposit after accepting a request; others collect no payment through Tími. When required, the exact amount, free-cancellation window, refund/no-show rule, policy version, and clinic name are shown before you authorize payment.

    Unless the displayed clinic policy says otherwise, the full deposit is credited against that clinic’s final invoice. The clinic bills all remaining veterinary charges directly and decides insurance handling. Tími does not submit veterinary insurance claims. Card processing is provided by Stripe. Tími’s platform fee is settled with the clinic and is not a veterinary charge billed to insurance.

    Refunds follow the policy snapshot you accepted, subject to applicable law. To dispute a deposit, contact billing@clearkey.solutions and the clinic. Cancelling a Tími request does not itself cancel care already arranged directly with a clinic.

    Amounts collected through Tími include a $25 Tími service fee, charged at the time of service, unless the clinic has elected to pass the entire $50 service fee to its customers — see the Service Fee section.

    SERVICE FEE

    One flat fee per completed intake.

    Tími charges a total service fee of $50 for each completed intake. Under the standard arrangement, $25 of that fee is collected from the customer at the time of service and the remainder is deducted from the clinic’s payout.

    A clinic may instead elect to pass the entire $50 service fee to its customers. Where a clinic has made that election, the full amount is disclosed to the customer at checkout, before payment is authorized.

    The service fee is a platform charge for intake routing and settlement. It is not a veterinary charge, is not part of the clinic’s invoice for care, and is not billed to insurance. It applies only to completed intakes — declined, expired, and cancelled requests carry no service fee.
